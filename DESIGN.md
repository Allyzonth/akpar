## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the regal heritage and professional standard of premier Indonesian hospitality, culinary arts, and tourism education. Rooted in the storied Majapahit lineage and translated for modern global education, the aesthetic conveys prestige, cultural warmth, culinary discipline, and academic distinction. 

The visual language balances **Editorial Elegance** with **Refined Contemporary Modernism**:
- **Prestigious & Royal:** Deep violet-purple anchors hero moments, institutional milestones, and footer foundations, evoking imperial history and academic reverence.
- **Warm & Hospitable:** Warm ivory and soft beige backdrops create an inviting canvas reminiscent of fine linen, batik parchment, and premium boutique resort interiors, avoiding the sterile chill of stark institutional whites.
- **Accented with Honor:** Rich Majapahit gold and focused terracotta-heritage red reflect the sunbeam crest emblem, artisanal copperware, and ancient red-brick temple monuments (Candi Bajang Ratu).
- **Target Audience:** Aspiring tourism managers, professional chefs, prospective international students, corporate hotel partners, and alumni seeking an institution that honors heritage while offering internationally certified hospitality training.

## Colors

The color palette translates the academy's traditional crest into a modern digital experience:

- **Primary (`#351047` - Royal Purple):** The institutional core. Used for master brand headers, primary call-to-action buttons, high-impact typography, dark hero sections, and solid footer blocks. It represents legacy, luxury hospitality management, and executive distinction.
- **Secondary (`#F6A51A` - Majapahit Gold):** The radiant accent. Used for key conversion highlights, accreditation badges, ratings, active navigation states, and refined decorative rules. It conveys warmth, prestige, and culinary excellence.
- **Tertiary (`#E52828` - Heritage Red):** A deliberate, low-frequency historical accent derived from the central temple emblem. Applied strictly to live application alerts, priority intake deadlines, and key historic callouts.
- **Neutral (`#27232B` - Charcoal):** High-contrast, warm dark neutral for editorial body copy, ensuring effortless reading without optical harshness.
- **Supporting Surface Colors:**
  - `Warm Ivory (#FFFDF8)`: Default canvas and body background.
  - `Soft Beige (#F3EDE4)`: Alternating section bands, curricula modules, and card background depths.
  - `Pure White (#FFFFFF)`: Elevated surfaces, course comparison tables, and spotlight card containers.
  - `Muted Gray (#77717C)`: Secondary metadata, timestamps, course codes, and sub-captions.

## Typography

The typographical pairing unites classical editorial prestige with contemporary Indonesian accessibility:

- **Display & Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Brings refined luxury, editorial gravitas, and institutional authority to hero statements, division titles, and faculty headings. It bridges traditional Indonesian culture and global hospitality magazine layouts.
- **Body & Functional UI (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Provides pristine legibility, balanced geometry, and friendly digital usability across course catalogs, application forms, admission portals, and practical schedules.
- **Tracking & Proportion:** Display headlines use a subtle negative letter-spacing (`-0.015em`) for a compact editorial feel. Uppercase category eyebrows and badge tags use wide tracking (`+0.08em`) to maintain clarity against ivory and gold backdrops.

## Layout & Spacing

The design system employs a structured 12-column fluid grid system on desktop, an 8-column layout on tablet, and a 4-column layout on mobile devices.

- **Editorial Rhythm:** Generous vertical padding (`space-xl` and section spans up to `5rem`) allows editorial imagery of culinary training, five-star hotel suites, and botanical campus grounds room to breathe.
- **Asymmetric Offsets:** Hero and featured program sections frequently pair high-impact text in 7 columns alongside offset, overlapping imagery and stat cards occupying the remaining 5 columns.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - Mobile: `< 640px` (single-column flows, edge gutters reduced to `1rem`).
  - Tablet: `640px – 1024px` (adaptive 2-column program cards, collapsed side rails).
  - Desktop: `> 1024px` (full 12-column layout with max content constrain at `1280px`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy emphasizes organic hospitality warmth over harsh digital shadows:

- **Tonal Layering:** The foundational layer rests on Warm Ivory (`#FFFDF8`). Mid-tier content segments transition into Soft Beige (`#F3EDE4`), while interactive program cards and focal modules sit on Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Warm Ambient Shadow:** Surfaces utilize low-opacity, warm-tinted shadows rather than cold grays:
  - *Card Rest:* `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(53, 16, 71, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(39, 35, 43, 0.03)`
  - *Elevated / Floating Card:* `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(53, 16, 71, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(246, 165, 26, 0.06)`
- **Delicate Borders:** Hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(53, 16, 71, 0.08)` or `1px solid rgba(246, 165, 26, 0.25)`) frame credential cards, program summaries, and accreditation seals.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a soft, architectural geometry (`roundedness: 1`). Corners maintain an intentional, tailored finish reminiscent of architectural stonework, diplomas, and high-end hospitality menus rather than playful, overly rounded SaaS interfaces.

- **Base Radius:** `0.25rem (4px)` for form inputs, chips, and small tags.
- **Card & Modal Radius:** `0.5rem (8px)` to `0.75rem (12px)` for institutional program containers, visual showcases, and interactive application steps.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Restricted exclusively to status tags (e.g., "Pendaftaran Dibuka", "Akreditasi A") and micro-pills containing certification badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Institutional Button:** Solid Royal Purple background (`#351047`), crisp white typography (`#FFFFFF`), `0.25rem` radius, subtle gold border accent or hover transition (`background: #47175E`).
- **Secondary / Action Accent:** Solid Majapahit Gold (`#F6A51A`) with Royal Purple label (`#351047`), creating high-visibility conversion pathways for "Daftar Sekarang" (Apply Now) and campus tours.
- **Ghost / Editorial Outline:** Transparent surface with `1.5px` border in Royal Purple or Majapahit Gold, with gentle color fill on hover.

### Badges & Chips
- **Accreditation & Intake Chips:** Majapahit Gold tinted background (`rgba(246, 165, 26, 0.12)`) with deep bronze-gold text (`#8E5900`) and a `1px` subtle gold border.
- **Heritage & Deadline Chips:** Muted Heritage Red tint (`rgba(229, 40, 40, 0.08)`) with Heritage Red text (`#E52828`) for intake deadlines and limited-seat culinary masterclasses.

### Cards (Programs & Faculty)
- Built on Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a `1px solid rgba(53, 16, 71, 0.06)` border and warm ambient shadow.
- Feature an image container with a 4:3 or 16:9 ratio, an editorial Playfair Display headline, a concise curriculum description in Plus Jakarta Sans, and an integrated bottom row displaying degree type (e.g., "D3 Perhotelan", "D4 Destinasi Pariwisata") and tuition details.

### Form Inputs & Select Fields
- Warm Ivory or White backgrounds with subtle inset framing (`1px solid rgba(53, 16, 71, 0.15)`).
- Active/focused state illuminates the field with a Majapahit Gold border ring (`#F6A51A`) and a delicate drop shadow, maintaining accessibility without jarring visual leaps.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Royal Purple check mark with an active fill of `#351047`.
- Unchecked states use a soft border (`#77717C`) over Warm Ivory backgrounds.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Curriculum Schedule Table:** High-density, clean rows styled with alternate row banding in `#F3EDE4`, clear semester milestones, and download action links for syllabi.
- **Heritage Crest Watermark Section:** Decorative low-opacity (4%) vector motif inspired by the Majapahit sunburst and monument silhouette subtly positioned behind key faculty bios or institutional history timelines.
